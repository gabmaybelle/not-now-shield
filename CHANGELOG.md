# Changelog

## 1.3.0 for Minecraft 1.21.1

Not Now, Shield for Minecraft 1.21.1, on Fabric and on NeoForge.

- Axes strip logs, scrape copper and remove wax even with a shield in your off-hand. When there is nothing to change, the shield raises as normal.
- Your shield is drawn lower on your screen, resting or raised, so it covers less of your view. It still blocks exactly the same, and modded shields are lowered too.
- Hoes and shovels already work with a shield in 1.21.1, so this version leaves them alone.

**Which file:** `not-now-shield-fabric` for Fabric (Fabric Loader 0.15 or newer; Fabric API isn't needed), `not-now-shield-neoforge` for NeoForge 21.1 or newer. Both need Java 21.

## 1.3.0

- Not Now, Shield now comes as a NeoForge mod as well as a Fabric one, for Minecraft 26.2 and 26.3. Tools still change blocks with a shield in your off-hand, and your shield is still drawn lower. On NeoForge it needs nothing but NeoForge itself.
- The Fabric file is now named `not-now-shield-fabric-1.3.0.jar`.
