# Changelog

## 1.3.0

- Not Now, Shield now comes as a NeoForge mod as well as a Fabric one, for Minecraft 26.2 and 26.3. Tools still change blocks with a shield in your off-hand, and your shield is still drawn lower. On NeoForge it needs nothing but NeoForge itself.
- The Fabric file is now named `not-now-shield-fabric-1.3.0.jar`.

## 1.2.0

- Your shield is drawn lower on your screen, resting or raised, so it covers less of your view. It still blocks exactly the same, and modded shields are lowered too.
- A new icon: the pixel-art axe now has a shield behind it.

## 1.1.2

- First release under the MIT License, with a new pixel-art icon.
- No more "Error loading class" line in the log on 26.3.
- Mod Menu links to the source and the issue tracker.

## 1.1.1

- First release: axes work with a shield in your off-hand on Minecraft 26.2, and axes, hoes and shovels on 26.3, in one jar. Released under CC0.
